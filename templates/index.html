About

Heart Health Checker

Welcome to the Heart Health Checker! This application is a personal passion and hobby project developed by Aashar Pamidiparti to explore the fascinating intersection of machine learning, data preprocessing, and healthcare analytics.

While exploring core ML workflows—ranging from data cleaning and feature engineering to model training and evaluation—this project serves as a hands-on sandbox to understand how predictive algorithms interpret clinical indicators. The goal is to build an intuitive, interactive tool that bridges foundational machine learning concepts with practical, real-world utility.

Disclaimer: This tool is built strictly for educational, experimental, and hobbyist exploration. It is not intended to serve as a diagnostic tool, medical advice, or a substitute for professional healthcare evaluation.
